The World

Where It All Began

The world is broken — swallowed by endless floodwaters after the great climate catastrophe. Humanity escaped upward, building giant flying industrial cities above the clouds, living like gods while the earth below was forgotten.

✦

Lyra is a mechanic — one of thousands who keep the sky-cities alive, dreaming of legends whispered about what lies beneath the mist. One night, a single blue spark changes everything. She steals a scout glider and descends… into a hidden world of bioluminescent beauty, where ancient forests breathe, where the trees remember what humanity forgot.

✦

But she was not alone. Others follow — and the forest's guardian delivers a warning older than memory: "They must not find this place. Or we all die." Lyra must choose between her people… and the last light the world has left.
